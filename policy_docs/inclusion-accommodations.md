# Inclusion and Accommodations Policy

## Commitment

Teams perform best with diverse perspectives and genuine access. This policy covers equal opportunity, reasonable accommodations, and employee resource groups. It applies to hiring, promotion, pay, leave administration, and daily conduct alike.

## Equal opportunity

Employment decisions rest on role-related criteria only. Job postings use inclusive language and essential-function descriptions. Interview panels mix roles and levels per the Hiring Policy. Pay reviews include an equity check across comparable roles.

## Reasonable accommodations

Employees may request accommodations for disability, pregnancy, or religious practice through HR with a short written description of the barrier and the adjustment sought. Medical documentation is limited to functional needs, never full history. HR responds within 5 business days with the determination or a trial arrangement.

## Accommodation examples

Adjusted schedules, screen-reader compatible tools, captioning on all-hands recordings, quiet workspace options, modified equipment, and leave-pattern flexibility. Each is granted unless it causes undue hardship, documented with the specific hardship reasoning when denied. Denials include the appeal path to the HR director.

## Religious observance

Schedule adjustments and floating-holiday use cover observances; see Holidays and Leave. Dress-code exceptions for religious garments are granted as standard. Meeting planners avoid major observances where the published calendar makes them known.

## Employee resource groups

Groups form with 5 founding members, a written charter, and an executive sponsor. They receive a modest annual budget, meeting space, and a leadership-development track for organizers. Groups advise (never decide) on policy reviews affecting their communities.

## Accessibility standards

Internal tools must meet baseline accessibility: keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, and captioned media. New tool procurement includes an accessibility check by IT. Retrofits are prioritized by employee impact with dated remediation plans.

## Records

Accommodation requests and outcomes are confidential medical-adjacent records with restricted access, retained 4 years. Aggregate accommodation volumes publish yearly with no names. Retaliation for requesting accommodation is a serious conduct violation.

## Frequently asked questions

Do I need a diagnosis on file? Functional needs only, never the full record. Can accommodations be temporary? Yes, with review dates set at grant time. Who pays for assistive tech? The company, through the Equipment Policy exception path.

## Interactive process in detail

Step 1: written request describing the barrier and candidate adjustments. Step 2: a collaborative meeting within 5 business days exploring options, including trial arrangements. Step 3: written determination with the granted terms, review dates, and appeal path. Interim measures apply from step 1 where the barrier blocks current work.

## Examples of granted adjustments

A recurring captioning setup for all-hands plus team ceremonies. A schedule shift overlapping core hours differently for treatment appointments. A screen-reader compatible workflow with tooling swaps where the default stack fails. A quiet-zone desk assignment with the same team rituals kept intact.

## Hiring and promotion fairness checks

Panels follow the Hiring Policy mix rules. Accommodation requests from candidates (extra time, accessible formats, adjusted exercises) are granted as standard with 3 business days notice. Promotion slates get an HR fairness review before calibration closes.

## Pay equity reviews

Annual reviews compare base, bonus, and promotion velocity across comparable roles with documented factor controls (level, function, location, tenure). Unexplained gaps trigger individual remediation plus process fixes. Aggregate results go to leadership; individual corrections are communicated privately with back-pay where owed.

## Definitions glossary

Reasonable accommodation is the adjustment granted barring documented undue hardship. Interactive process is the request-meeting-determination flow with 5-day meeting turnaround. Interim measures apply from request day where work is blocked. Special enrollment is the 30-day post-event benefits window. Undue hardship is the specific documented burden justifying a denial, appealable to the HR director. Resource group is the 5-member chartered community with sponsor and budget.
