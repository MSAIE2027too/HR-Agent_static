# Approval Matrix

## Who approves what

PTO requests: manager, with automatic balance check first. Requests of 3 or more consecutive days always need the manager regardless of balance. Blackout-period PTO needs VP approval. Same-week requests get a 2-business-day decision.

Remote work: manager plus Security sign-off, with tax review in parallel. Beyond 6 weeks: VP approval in addition. International stays over 30 days: Finance clearance. Standard decisions issue within 5 business days.

Expenses: manager approval for all items; amounts of $500 or more additionally need Finance. Laptop exceptions for non-engineers: manager approval case by case. Home-office furniture: manager approval for remote and hybrid employees. Advances: manager approval 2 weeks ahead.

Benefits: HR confirms eligibility and enrollment windows; managers are not in the path. Life-event windows open on HR receipt of documentation. Retirement changes need no approval at all.

Equipment: manager approval for all items; IT confirms stock and fulfills. Returns are handled by IT on departure with no approval needed. Software licenses need manager approval plus a cost center.

Travel: manager approval for all trips; international trips additionally need Finance; per-diem overages need Finance pre-approval. Cancellations need no approval but immediate action.

Leave of absence: HR determines with documentation; managers are informed, not approvers, except unpaid personal leave which needs both. Extensions follow the same path before expiry.

Conduct cases: HR owns triage and investigation; managers implement interim measures on HR instruction only. Reporters get acknowledgment in 2 business days.

Compensation: annual reviews run the standard cycle; off-cycle adjustments need VP and HR approval. Overtime needs manager pre-approval.

## Ticket and case routing

HR tickets created from chat drafts route to the HR queue with the policy citations attached. Mock tickets in this system are clearly labeled and take no real action. Sensitive cases route directly to HR, never to the requester's own manager alone. Case numbers issue within 2 business days of filing.

## Turnaround standards

Standard approvals: 5 business days. Same-week PTO: 2 business days. Security sign-off: 5 business days. HR determinations: 5 business days. Conduct acknowledgment: 2 business days; investigation: 10 business days where feasible. Missed standards escalate automatically to the next level with notice to the requester.

## Denials and appeals

Every denial states the sections relied upon and what would change the outcome. Appeals go to the next level within the window stated in the denial (default 2 weeks). Appeal decisions are final except for conduct cases, which allow one HR-director review.

## Cross-policy approval chains

Remote work chain: employee files with location, dates, overlap, manager name → manager coverage check → Security device and destination check → Finance tax review in parallel → decision in 5 business days → VP added only beyond 6 weeks. PTO chain: portal balance check → coverage acknowledgment → manager decision, with VP added only for blackouts and 2-day decisions for same-week filings. Expense chain: filer with receipts and purpose → eligibility screen → manager → Finance at $500+, reimbursement in 2 pay cycles. Travel chain: portal booking → manager → Finance for international or overages → trip record opened for receipts. Equipment chain: portal request with justification → manager → IT stock confirmation → tracked shipment. Leave chain: portal filing with documents → HR determination in 5 days → manager informed (except unpaid personal needing both). Conduct chain: any channel → HR triage in 2 days → investigation in 10 where feasible → summary to reporter. Hiring chain: requisition → loop with 24-hour scorecards → decision in 5 days → offer with VP+HR on adjustments. Termination chain: plan completion or misconduct record → VP+HR business case → severance packet with 21-day consideration.

## Definitions glossary

Balance check is the automatic PTO sufficiency verification before approval. Sign-off is the required second approval from Security, Finance, or VP. Parallel review means approvers act simultaneously rather than in sequence. Turnaround standard is the published decision deadline per request class. Interim measures are temporary arrangements during conduct review. Mock ticket is the clearly labeled no-action draft the assistant creates only after confirmation.

## Worked routing scenarios

Scenario A: a 4-day PTO request with balance verified and coverage acknowledged routes manager-only and decides in 5 business days. Scenario B: a 7-week remote request routes manager, Security, and tax in parallel plus VP for the excess week; missing VP attachment returns it as incomplete, not denied. Scenario C: a $1,200 equipment exception routes manager then Finance with the business reason attached; denial names the missing justification.

## Delegation rules

Managers going on leave name a delegate with written scope and dates; the delegate's decisions carry full weight and are logged under both names. Delegations over 2 weeks need the next level informed. No one approves their own request, their delegate's request about them, or any case where they are a party.

## Emergency approvals

Genuine emergencies (safety, travel disruption, system outage) allow verbal approval followed by written confirmation within 2 business days. The confirmer records what was decided, why waiting was impossible, and which sections applied. Abuse of the emergency path is a conduct matter.

## Records and retention

Every approval, denial, delegation, and appeal is logged with approver, date, sections relied upon, and outcome. Records live 3 years (pay records 4, conduct records per case policy). Requesters can view their own history anytime; it ships within 5 business days.
