# Holidays and Leave

## Observed holidays

The company observes New Year, Spring Break Friday, Independence Week, Thanksgiving (2 days), and Winter Closure (Dec 24–Jan 1). Observed closures are paid and do not consume PTO. When a holiday falls on a weekend, the nearest Friday or Monday is observed. The published calendar for the year ships each November.

## Floating holiday

Each employee receives 1 floating holiday per year for personal, cultural, or religious observance, usable with 1 week notice and manager approval. It does not carry over. New hires joining after July receive it immediately; the rule is the same for everyone.

## Holiday time off

Holiday time off needs no ticket. Staff required for holiday coverage are scheduled 1 month in advance and receive equivalent time off in lieu within the following quarter. Volunteers for coverage are taken first; assignments rotate yearly for fairness.

## Family emergency leave

Up to 5 paid days per event for family emergencies. Provide reasonable documentation (for example a note or booking confirmation) within 2 weeks of return. Give written notice to your manager before or within 24 hours of absence. PTO may be elected to extend the absence. Separate events each carry their own 5-day allowance.

## Bereavement

3 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member, 1 day for extended family. Notice as soon as practicable; no documentation required unless the absence exceeds 3 days. Extensions draw from PTO or unpaid personal leave with manager approval.

## Request process

Emergency leave needs a short written notice to your manager before or within 24 hours of absence. Planned holiday-adjacent PTO follows the PTO Policy advance-notice rules. All emergency notices get a same-day acknowledgment.

## School and civic appointments

Up to 2 half-days per year for school conferences or civic appointments with 1 week notice. These do not draw from PTO. Voting leave of 2 hours applies where polls are open less than 4 hours outside shift time.

## Frequently asked questions

Do contractors get paid closures? No, contractor terms govern. What if an emergency strikes during approved PTO? The overlapping days convert to emergency leave with documentation, preserving PTO. Who covers holiday support? The published rotation; see Holiday time off.

## Coverage rotation administration

The rotation roster publishes each November with the holiday calendar. Swaps are allowed with both parties' written agreement and manager acknowledgment. No one covers two consecutive major holidays unless volunteering. Time in lieu must be scheduled within the following quarter or it converts to a floating credit with manager approval.

## Emergency leave administration

HR logs every emergency leave with dates and documentation status. Missing documentation at the 2-week mark triggers a reminder, not discipline; at 30 days the days convert to PTO or unpaid with written notice and appeal rights. Managers may not ask for diagnosis details, only for the documentation class the policy names.

## Worked examples

Example A: a 3-day family emergency with a booking confirmation filed on return. Paid as emergency leave, no PTO touched, documentation complete on day 2.

Example B: bereavement of 2 days for an immediate family member. Paid in full, no documentation needed, manager notified the same morning.

Example C: a holiday-week PTO request adjacent to Winter Closure. Closure days are paid and skipped in the PTO count; only the non-closure days draw from the balance.

## Definitions glossary

Observed closure is a paid company-wide shutdown day that consumes neither PTO nor remote weeks. Floating holiday is the single personal observance day usable with 1 week notice. Time in lieu is compensatory time off for holiday coverage, schedulable within the following quarter. Emergency leave is the 5-day-per-event paid allowance with 24-hour notice and 2-week documentation. Bereavement is the 3-day (immediate) or 1-day (extended) paid allowance. Blackout-adjacent PTO is planned time off touching a closure, counted only on non-closure days.

## Calendar administration

The yearly calendar publishes each November with observed dates, closure spans, and blackout windows. Corrections (for example a shifted observance) publish with 1 month notice and never retroactively. Managers reconcile team PTO balances against the calendar in December planning.
