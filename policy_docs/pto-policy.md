# PTO Policy

## Accrual

Employees accrue 15 PTO days per year, credited at 1.25 days per month. New hires may use accrued days from the first month; unaccrued advance days need manager approval. Accrual pauses during unpaid leave exceeding 30 days. Accrual resumes the first full pay period after return.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next calendar year. Carried days must be used by end of March or they expire. Days beyond the 5-day cap are forfeited at year end. Managers receive a January report of team carryover balances to encourage planning.

## Request process

Submit PTO requests at least 1 week in advance with dates and a coverage plan naming who handles urgent work. Requests of 3 or more consecutive days need manager approval regardless of balance. Same-week requests are decided within 2 business days; earlier requests within 5. The portal shows the balance check result before submission completes.

## Manager approval

Managers approve based on team coverage, blackout periods, and remaining balance. Balances are checked automatically before approval, and requests exceeding the remaining balance are returned for revision. Approval decisions include the policy section relied upon. Split approvals (part of the dates) must state which dates are approved.

## Blackout periods

Two blackout windows apply company-wide: the Winter Closure week and the end-of-quarter release week. PTO during blackouts needs VP approval. Team-specific blackouts are published quarterly with 1 month notice. Blackout calendars live in the HR portal; ignorance of a published blackout is not grounds for appeal.

## Coverage plans

Every request of 3 or more days names a coverage owner with documented handoff notes. The coverage owner must acknowledge before approval issues. For single-day absences a brief out-of-office note with an escalation contact suffices.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Notify your manager before shift start. No balance deduction for the first 5 sick days per year; beyond that, PTO or medical leave applies. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick days cannot be converted to PTO payout.

## Coordination with other leave

PTO runs concurrently with family emergency leave only where the employee elects it. Parental, medical, and jury leave are separate; see the Leave of Absence Policy. Holiday closures do not consume PTO. Floating holidays are separate from PTO; see Holidays and Leave.

## Payout on departure

Accrued unused PTO up to 10 days pays out on voluntary departure; carried-over days do not pay out. Forfeited cap-excess days never pay out. Payout appears in the final paycheck with an itemized stub.

## Definitions glossary

Accrual is the 1.25 days earned monthly toward the 15-day year. Advance days are unaccrued days usable only with manager approval. Carryover is the max 5 unused days transferable to next year, due by March. Blackout is a no-PTO window (Winter Closure week, release week, published team windows) except with VP approval. Coverage owner is the named acknowledger of handoff notes on 3+ day requests. Same-week request is decided in 2 business days. Forfeiture is the year-end loss of cap-excess and expired carried days.

## Frequently asked questions

Can PTO be taken in half days? Yes, in half-day increments with the same approval rules. Can a denied request be appealed? Yes, to the manager's manager within 1 week, with the coverage plan attached. Does remote work consume PTO? No, remote days are work days under the Remote Work Policy.

## Accrual worked examples

A January joiner accrues 1.25 days monthly and holds 3.75 days by end of March, all usable immediately. A July joiner holds 7.5 days by year end and may carry 5 into the next year, with the carried days due by March. Advance days beyond the accrued balance need manager approval noted on the request.

## Request triage order

The portal checks in this order: blackout status, balance sufficiency, coverage acknowledgment, then manager decision. A failure at any step returns the request with the specific reason and the section to read, so resubmission is one round trip, not several.

## Team coordination norms

Teams publish vacation calendars quarterly. No more than a third of a team may overlap absences without VP approval. Managers may suggest alternate dates but may not deny without a coverage-based reason recorded on the request.

## Abuse and edge cases

Patterned Monday/Friday absences trigger a coverage review, not discipline, as the first step. Retroactive PTO for past dates needs manager approval within 1 week and never for blackout dates. PTO cannot be donated or transferred between employees.

## Manager playbook

Publish the team vacation calendar and blackout dates at quarter start. Decide same-week requests in 2 business days and others in 5; silence is not a decision. Write denials with the coverage reason and two alternate windows. Track carryover in January and nudge holders before March expiry. Never ask for diagnosis details on sick days; the notification and the doctor's-note rule are the whole process. Escalate repeated blackout appeals to VP with the coverage math attached.

## Leave-year calendar administration

The leave year runs January to December with balances resetting after carryover processing in the second January week. The portal freezes requests during the 3-day processing window with advance notice. January statements show prior-year use, carryover granted, expiry deadlines, and the fresh 15-day accrual schedule. Mid-year joiners see prorated schedules with the same statement format.
