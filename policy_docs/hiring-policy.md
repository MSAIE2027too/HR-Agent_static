# Hiring Policy

## Requisition process

Managers open requisitions with the role definition, band, budget approval, and interview panel named. Finance confirms headcount; HR confirms the band against market data. Requisitions older than 90 days without activity close automatically and may reopen on request.

## Interview stages

Standard loop: recruiter screen, hiring-manager interview, panel round (3 to 4 sessions), and a final values conversation. Panels mix roles and levels; every interviewer files a scorecard within 24 hours. Skills assessments are paid work samples under 3 hours, never free labor on production systems.

## Scorecards and decisions

Scorecards rate role skills, collaboration signals, and growth trajectory on the 4-point scale with written evidence per mark. The hiring manager decides with the panel's input; split panels escalate to the skip-level with all scorecards attached. Decisions issue within 5 business days of the final round.

## Offers

Offers state base, band, bonus target, benefits start, and start date. Candidate-requested adjustments need VP and HR approval. Exploding offers under 5 business days are prohibited. Background checks run post-acceptance and cover employment, education, and role-relevant records only.

## Referrals

Employee referrals earn a bonus paid half at hire and half at 6 months, provided the referrer stays employed. Referrers may not interview their own referrals. Self-reported referral fraud forfeits the bonus and triggers a conduct review.

## Internal mobility

Internal candidates apply after 1 year in role with manager notification (not permission). They skip the recruiter screen and get feedback within 1 week of each stage. Rejected internal candidates receive growth notes and a 6-month revisit window.

## Records

Requisitions, scorecards, decisions, and offers are retained 3 years. Candidate data is never shared outside the panel. Rejected candidates may request their timeline (not others' scorecards) within 30 days.

## Frequently asked questions

Can contractors convert? Yes, through the standard loop with tenure credit for band placement. Do referrals guarantee interviews? They guarantee a screen, not a loop. Who approves off-band offers? VP plus HR with market evidence attached.

## Sourcing and pipeline standards

Every loop draws from at least two sourcing channels, with referral, inbound, and outbound shares tracked for pipeline health. Time-to-decision targets: screen within 1 week of application, loop within 3 weeks, decision within 5 business days of the final round. Stale requisitions get a pipeline review before extension.

## Assessment design rules

Work samples mirror real tasks at reduced scope, cap at 3 hours, and are never used in production. Take-home and live exercises are both allowed; live exercises need a structured rubric shared with the candidate in advance. Prohibited content includes unpaid production work, brainteasers without job relevance, and questions touching protected characteristics.

## Onboarding handoff

The hiring manager delivers a 30/60/90 plan, the buddy assignment, and the equipment request before day 1, per the Onboarding Policy. Offer details (band, bonus target, start date) transfer verbatim into the HR record so payroll and benefits match what was promised. Mismatches found in week 1 are corrected retroactively.

## Hiring metrics and audits

Quarterly audits cover time-to-fill, offer-accept rate, panel diversity, and 1-year new-hire performance correlation. Scorecard completion under 24 hours is tracked per interviewer. Panels with chronic no-show or late-scorecard patterns lose interview privileges until retrained.

## Definitions glossary

Requisition is the approved hiring request with role, band, budget, and panel. Scorecard is the per-interviewer 24-hour evaluation with written evidence. Work sample is the capped 3-hour paid task, never production labor. Split panel is a divided decision escalated to skip-level with all scorecards. Exploding offer is a sub-5-day deadline, prohibited. Referral bonus pays half at hire and half at 6 months. Internal mobility is the 1-year-tenure transfer path with notification, not permission.
