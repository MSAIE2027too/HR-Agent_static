# Onboarding and Conduct

## Onboarding checklist

New hires complete three items within the first week: equipment request, account setup, and policy acknowledgment. Day 1 covers accounts and security training; day 2 to 3 covers equipment and tool access; day 4 to 5 covers policy acknowledgment and team introductions. A buddy is assigned for the first month, meeting weekly.

## Equipment request

Laptop plus peripherals on request with manager approval, per the Equipment Policy. Requests filed during onboarding week are fulfilled within 5 business days. Remote hires receive shipment tracking automatically. Missing items are reported to IT within 48 hours of delivery.

## Account setup

IT provisions email, chat, code, and HR-portal access within 24 hours of the signed offer. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all accounts from day 1. Access reviews run quarterly. Day-1 logins that fail route to the IT queue with same-day response.

## Policy acknowledgment

New hires acknowledge the code of conduct, data security rules, and leave policies in the HR portal. Acknowledgment is a precondition for equipment handoff and system access beyond read-only. Managers verify completion in the week-1 check-in.

## First-month milestones

Week 2: first ticket or deliverable with buddy review. Week 3: benefits enrollment completed. Week 4: 30-day check-in with the manager covering role clarity, workload, and open blockers. Probation is 3 months with a written midpoint review.

## Workplace conduct

All employees follow the code of conduct; see the Workplace Conduct Policy for definitions, reporting channels, and investigation timelines. Violations go to HR triage: minor issues get coaching, serious issues get a formal case. Managers must escalate anything beyond coaching-level to HR within 2 business days.

## Sensitive issues and escalation

Report sensitive workplace issues to HR directly. The assistant retrieves relevant policy, recommends escalation where appropriate, and can draft a mock HR case summary. It never takes irreversible action without confirmation.

## Data security incidents

Disconnect, preserve logs, and notify Security immediately, per the Data Security Policy. Do not forward or exfiltrate customer data during investigation. New hires complete the incident-response drill in week 1.

## Frequently asked questions

What if equipment arrives late? Loaners ship within 2 business days on IT ticket. Can onboarding be remote? Yes, with video sessions and shipped equipment; policy acknowledgment works identically. Who is my buddy if they leave? The manager reassigns within 1 week.

## Manager duties

Managers own the week-1 check-in, the 30-day review, and confirmation that acknowledgment, equipment, and access all landed. They introduce the buddy, set the first deliverable, and name the escalation path for blockers. Missed milestones trigger support, not blame, as the first response.

## 30/60/90 milestones

Day 30: role clarity confirmed, first deliverable shipped, benefits enrolled. Day 60: independent ticket ownership, security drill completed, feedback exchanged both ways. Day 90: probation review with written outcome — confirm, extend once with a plan, or part ways with notice and payout per policy.

## Offboarding mirror

Departures reverse onboarding: access revoked day-last, equipment returned within 2 weeks, knowledge transfer documented in the final week. Exit interviews are offered to all, mandatory for managers to request. Final pay includes PTO payout per the PTO Policy and any cleared expenses.

## Records

Acknowledgments, check-in notes, and probation outcomes live in the HR portal for the employment duration plus 3 years. Buddies keep no formal records; their role is guidance, not evaluation.

## Definitions glossary

Policy acknowledgment is the portal confirmation unlocking equipment and full access. Buddy is the assigned peer guide for the first month. Thirty-day review is the manager check-in on role clarity and blockers. Probation is the 3-month period with a written midpoint review. Triage is HR's classification of conduct matters into coaching or formal tracks. Read-only access is the pre-acknowledgment system state.

## Offboarding administration

Exit interviews are scheduled in the final week with a standard question set; themes publish quarterly anonymized. Knowledge transfer uses the living handoff template: systems, contacts, risks, and next steps. Alumni references follow the Termination Policy title-and-dates rule with no exceptions for tenure or seniority.
