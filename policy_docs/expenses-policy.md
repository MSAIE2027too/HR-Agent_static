# Expense Policy

## Reimbursable items

Laptops for Engineers and home-office chairs for remote or hybrid employees are reimbursable with manager approval. Travel follows the Travel Policy. Standard peripherals (keyboard, mouse, monitor) are covered under the Equipment Policy rather than expenses. Wellness claims follow the Benefits Policy.

## Role and location eligibility

Eligibility is checked against role and work location before approval. Engineers qualify for laptop purchases; Designers and Analysts use assigned workstations, with laptop exceptions granted case by case with manager approval based on travel or remote needs. Remote and hybrid employees qualify for home-office furniture; onsite employees use office facilities. Eligibility disputes go to Finance with the role matrix attached.

## Thresholds and receipts

Expenses over $50 need an itemized receipt. Reports are due within 30 days of purchase. Amounts under $500 need manager approval only; amounts of $500 or more additionally need Finance approval. Split purchases to evade thresholds are a conduct violation. Estimates are never accepted in place of receipts above $50.

## Submission

File reports with receipts, business purpose, and cost center. Finance reimburses within 2 pay cycles. Incomplete reports are returned once with notes; resubmit within 2 weeks. Reports older than 90 days are denied except with VP approval and documented cause.

## Non-reimbursable items

Commuting costs, personal devices without business justification, fines and penalties, and coworking memberships (see Remote Work Policy) are not reimbursable. Alcohol is reimbursable only for client entertainment with VP approval. Gift cards and cash equivalents are never reimbursable.

## Audits

Finance audits a sample of reports quarterly. Keep receipts for 1 year. Rejected claims include the policy section relied upon so employees can correct and resubmit. Repeat violations trigger manager notification and possible revocation of direct-filing privileges.

## Advances

Travel advances up to the estimated trip cost are available with manager approval 2 weeks ahead. Advances clear against the filed report within the same 2-pay-cycle window. Uncleared advances over 60 days are deducted from pay with written notice.

## Definitions glossary

Itemized receipt shows vendor, date, line items, and total; card slips alone do not qualify above $50. Business purpose is the one-line reason tying the spend to work. Cost center is the accounting bucket charged. Per diem is the daily meals allowance on travel days. Advance is pre-trip cash cleared by the later report. Threshold-split is dividing one purchase to evade approval tiers, classified as fraud on repeat.

## Filing walkthrough

Step 1: photograph receipts at purchase; the portal reads dates and totals. Step 2: pick the category; the portal applies the right thresholds and routes manager-only or manager-plus-Finance. Step 3: write the business purpose naming the project or trip. Step 4: submit and watch the tracker; returned reports show the exact defect and the section to read. Resubmit within 2 weeks.

## Manager review checklist

Verify the person, the business purpose, and the receipt math. Check role and location eligibility before the amount. Confirm no threshold-split across adjacent reports from the same trip. Approve or return within 5 business days; silence past the SLA escalates to Finance with notice to the filer.

## Frequently asked questions

Can I expense a monitor for home? Yes, through the Equipment Policy path, not expenses. What if a receipt is lost? Card statements plus a written attestation work once per year; repeat losses are denied. Do client dinners need attendee lists? Yes, names and business purpose for any meal over $50.

## Category guide

Office supplies under $200 need receipts but no pre-approval. Software under $500 per year follows the Equipment Policy license checklist. Mileage reimburses at the published annual rate with origin, destination, and purpose logged. Phone plans are not expensed; the company issues devices where the role requires on-call availability.

## Fraud signals and consequences

Duplicate receipts, altered dates, personal items bundled with business ones, and threshold-splitting are reviewed as fraud, not error, on the second occurrence. First occurrences get documented coaching. Confirmed fraud repays the amount and faces conduct proceedings up to termination.

## Worked examples

Example A: a remote Engineer buys a $350 chair with an itemized receipt and manager approval. Role and location checks pass, amount is under $500, reimbursement lands within 2 pay cycles.

Example B: an onsite Analyst submits a $1,200 laptop without prior approval. The laptop exception path requires case-by-case manager approval first, and the amount needs Finance too. The claim returns with both missing steps named.

Example C: a $60 client dinner with attendee names and VP approval for the single glass of wine. Compliant as filed.
