# Equipment Policy

## Eligibility matrix

Engineers receive laptops as standard issue. Designers and Analysts are assigned workstations, with laptop exceptions granted case by case with manager approval based on travel or remote needs. Remote and hybrid employees additionally qualify for home-office peripherals. Exceptions cite the business reason in the request.

## Standard issue

Laptops refresh on a 3-year cycle; peripherals (keyboard, mouse, monitor, headset) refresh on request with manager approval. Requests filed during onboarding week ship within 5 business days; standard requests within 2 weeks. Backorders are communicated with revised dates, not silence.

## Home-office furniture

Home-office chairs are reimbursable for remote and hybrid employees under the Expense Policy with manager approval. Desks and lighting follow the same path with a combined cap of $800 per 3 years. Onsite employees use office facilities instead. Keep receipts per the $50 rule.

## Request flow

File in the equipment portal with business justification and manager name. Manager approval is required for all items; IT confirms stock and ships with tracking. Denials include the reason and an alternative where one exists. Escalate stalled requests to IT leadership after 3 weeks.

## Returns and offboarding

All company equipment must be returned within 2 weeks of departure, using the prepaid label. Unreturned items are invoiced at depreciated value after 30 days. Device wipe is performed by IT on receipt. Remote departures get doorstep pickup where available.

## Loss and damage

Report loss within 24 hours; accidental damage is covered once per refresh cycle, negligence is reviewed case by case. Never attempt self-repair on encrypted drives. Spill and drop incidents need an IT ticket the same day for warranty purposes.

## Software and licenses

Standard software is preinstalled; special licenses need manager approval and a cost center. Open-source tools are welcome but must pass the license checklist for copyleft terms. Unapproved admin installs are removed in quarterly sweeps.

## Frequently asked questions

Can I keep the old laptop at refresh? No, refreshes swap; buyout is unavailable. What about monitors for hybrid days at home? One home monitor is covered under peripherals. Who pays for faster home internet? The employee; no internet stipend exists.

## Lifecycle timeline

Year 0: standard issue at onboarding. Year 1 to 2: peripherals on request, battery service as needed. Year 3: refresh eligibility opens automatically with a portal prompt. Off-cycle replacement needs IT confirmation of failure plus manager approval. Loaners cover every gap: onboarding delays, repairs, and loss cases ship within 2 business days.

## Loaner pool

The loaner pool holds laptops and core peripherals for temporary needs. Loans run 30 days, renewable once with IT approval. Borrowers accept the same encryption, VPN, and loss-reporting duties as assigned equipment. Unreturned loaners convert to assigned inventory or invoices per the returns rule.

## Exceptions log

Every nonstandard grant (analyst laptop, extra monitor, early refresh) records role, reason, approver, and date. IT reviews the log quarterly for pattern drift into shadow standards; drifted patterns become formal eligibility proposals rather than quiet precedent.

## Disposal

Retired devices go through certified wipe or destruction with certificates retained 3 years. Employee purchase of retired gear is unavailable to avoid data-risk edge cases. Donations of wiped peripherals run through the approved program yearly.

## Depreciation schedule

Laptops depreciate straight-line over 3 years to zero; peripherals over 2 years. Unreturned gear invoices at the depreciated value with the schedule attached so the math is checkable. Lost-device invoices use the same schedule minus the covered first incident per refresh cycle. Disputes go to IT with purchase records reviewed within 2 weeks.

## Stipend vs purchase rules

No cash stipends substitute for portal fulfillment: chairs, desks, and lighting route through the Expense Policy with receipts; monitors and input devices route through equipment issue. Internet service is never subsidized in any form. Exceptions to channel rules need IT leadership approval with the reason logged in the exceptions log.

## Definitions glossary

Standard issue is the role-based default kit (laptop for Engineers, workstation otherwise). Refresh cycle is the 3-year replacement clock opened by automatic portal prompt. Loaner pool is the 30-day temporary equipment stock, renewable once. Exception grant is a documented nonstandard approval with role, reason, approver, and date. Depreciation value is the age-adjusted price used for unreturned-gear invoices after 30 days.

## Stock and fulfillment administration

IT publishes stock levels monthly; backordered items show revised dates on the request itself. Onboarding-week requests jump the queue automatically. Stalled requests escalate to IT leadership at 3 weeks with the full history attached. Fulfillment times are reported quarterly against the 5-day onboarding and 2-week standard targets.
