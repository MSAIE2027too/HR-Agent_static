# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed onboarding are eligible to request remote work. Part-time employees may request up to 2 weeks per year with manager approval. Contractors follow the terms in their own agreements, not this policy. Employees on a performance improvement plan are ineligible until the plan closes successfully.

## Duration limits

Employees may work remotely up to 6 weeks per calendar year from an approved location. Any request beyond 6 weeks needs written VP approval. Unused weeks do not carry over to the next year. The cap counts all remote days, including extensions of business trips (see Travel Policy).

## Request process

Submit a remote-work request at least 2 weeks in advance with the location, exact dates, time-zone overlap plan, and manager name. Requests of 2 weeks or more additionally need a coverage plan naming who handles urgent matters. The portal confirms receipt immediately and routes to the manager, Security, and tax review in parallel.

## Approval process

Manager approval plus Security sign-off are both required before travel. The manager confirms coverage and performance standing; Security confirms device compliance and data-handling rules for the destination. Tax review runs in parallel (see Tax and location rules). Approval or denial is issued within 5 business days, citing the sections relied upon. Denials include the reason and what would change the decision.

## Security requirements

Remote work must use the company VPN and device encryption. Customer data may not be stored on personal devices. Public Wi-Fi is prohibited without VPN. Screen lock must engage within 5 minutes. Report data incidents to Security immediately, following the Data Security Policy. Annual security training must be current before any international remote stint. Lost devices abroad follow the 24-hour reporting rule with remote wipe.

## Tax and location rules

Work from another state or country triggers a tax-location review. The employee must submit the location and dates at least 2 weeks in advance. Stays over 30 days in one jurisdiction need Finance clearance for payroll-tax implications. Work from sanctioned countries is prohibited. Finance confirms the determination in writing before travel; traveling without clearance is a conduct issue.

## Equipment and workspace

Remote workers use their assigned laptop plus requested peripherals under the Equipment Policy. Home-office chairs are reimbursable for remote and hybrid employees under the Expense Policy. The company does not fund coworking memberships except with VP approval. Ergonomic assessments are available on request through IT.

## International remote work

International requests need all standard approvals plus confirmation of right-to-work in the destination. The 6-week annual cap applies globally, not per country. Time-zone overlap of at least 4 working hours with the home team is required. The employee carries a printed approval summary and emergency contacts while abroad.

## Working hours and availability

Remote workers keep their home-team core hours with the agreed overlap, attend all scheduled ceremonies on video, and keep calendars current. Output is measured by deliverables and responsiveness, not keystrokes; no monitoring software is installed beyond standard device management.

## Revocation

Remote arrangements may be revoked for performance issues, security violations, or business need, with 1 week notice except in security emergencies, where access is suspended immediately. Revocation is documented with reasons and may be appealed to HR within 2 weeks. A second revocation within a year triggers a 6-month ineligibility period.

## Frequently asked questions

Can remote weeks be split into single days? Yes, partial weeks count pro rata against the 6-week cap. Does holiday closure while remote consume the cap? No, paid closures never consume remote weeks or PTO. Can two teammates swap coverage informally? Coverage plans must name names in the request; informal swaps need manager acknowledgment in writing.

## Manager playbook

Confirm overlap hours explicitly in the approval, not just dates. Check performance standing before coverage: struggling contributors need the improvement conversation first, not a remote yes. Verify Security sign-off landed before the employee travels; never pre-approve contingent on a future signature. During the stint, hold the normal ceremony cadence and review deliverables weekly. Document revocation reasons the same day; memory fades and appeals need records.

## Appeals in detail

Appeals state which finding is disputed and attach new evidence (coverage commitment, tax paperwork, corrected dates). HR decides within 2 weeks with the original approvers recused from the ruling. A successful appeal reissues the approval with the original effective dates; the lost time is not compensated beyond the restored arrangement. Second appeals go to the VP with the full record.

## Record retention schedule

Requests and decisions: 3 years. Tax determinations: 4 years with Finance. Revocations and appeals: 4 years. Denied incomplete requests: 1 year. Quarterly stats keep volume, median time, and reason codes with no names attached.

## Roles and responsibilities

The employee owns the request quality: complete dates, real overlap hours, and a tested VPN before departure. The manager owns the coverage decision and the performance standing check. Security owns device and destination risk. Finance owns the tax determination. HR owns appeals and revocation records. No party may approve its own request.

## Worked examples

Example A: an Engineer (Remote-US profile) requests 3 weeks from another state. Profile lookup confirms role and location, policy search returns duration, security, and tax sections, and the approval section yields manager plus Security sign-off with a 2-week filing. Cited next steps follow.

Example B: a 7-week request. The first 6 weeks follow the standard path; week 7 needs the written VP approval attached before Security signs. Without it the request returns as incomplete, not denied, so the employee can cure it.

Example C: a 10-day stay crossing into a second country mid-trip. Each country needs its own tax-location line in the request, and the 30-day Finance clearance clock runs per jurisdiction.

## Records

Approvals, denials, tax determinations, and revocations are stored 3 years. Employees keep the approval summary for the trip duration. HR publishes anonymized quarterly stats: volume, median decision time, and denial reasons.

## Definitions glossary

Approved location is the destination named in a granted request; working elsewhere voids coverage. Coverage plan names who handles urgent matters with documented handoff. Overlap hours are the minimum 4 shared working hours with the home team. Tax-location review is Finance's payroll-tax determination per jurisdiction. Security sign-off confirms device compliance and destination data rules. Revocation ends the arrangement with 1 week notice (immediate for security emergencies). Appeal is the 2-week HR challenge to a revocation. Sanctioned country is a prohibited destination regardless of approvals.
