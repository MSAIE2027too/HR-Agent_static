# Termination Policy

## Voluntary resignation

Give 2 weeks written notice; managers may waive the period with pay in lieu. The final 2 weeks focus on knowledge transfer with a documented handoff list. Unused PTO up to 10 days pays out per the PTO Policy; carried-over days do not.

## Involuntary process

Performance-based termination needs a completed improvement plan (see Performance Reviews) except for serious misconduct, which follows the conduct track with same-day suspension option. Role eliminations need VP and HR approval with a written business case and selection criteria review for bias.

## Severance

Role-elimination severance is 2 weeks per year of service, minimum 4 weeks, with benefits continuation through the severance period. Separation agreements include standard releases; employees get 21 days to consider and 7 to revoke, with HR available for questions throughout.

## Final pay and paperwork

Final paychecks include worked time, PTO payout, and cleared expenses, issued on the next cycle or earlier per local law. Departure packets cover benefits continuation election (30-day window), 401(k) rollover, reference policy, and equipment return labels.

## References

The company confirms title and dates only, through HR. Managers give no informal references. Candidates may authorize nothing broader; any broader request is declined as policy.

## Offboarding checklist

Access revoked on the last day, equipment due within 2 weeks, knowledge transfer signed off by the manager, exit interview offered. Remote departures get doorstep equipment pickup. Unreturned gear invoices at depreciated value after 30 days.

## Records

Termination records, plans, severance terms, and handoffs are retained 4 years with restricted access. Exit-interview themes publish quarterly in anonymized form to leadership.

## Exit interview program

Interviews run 30 minutes with a standard set: reasons for leaving, what worked, what would have retained, manager effectiveness, and process pain points. Participation is voluntary except for the HR-offered slot itself, which is mandatory to schedule. Notes are anonymized before theming; quotes need explicit permission. Theme reports go to leadership quarterly with trend lines and the top 3 requested changes tracked to resolution or written decline with reasons.

## Definitions glossary

Written notice is the 2-week email or portal resignation starting the clock on acknowledgment. Pay in lieu is the manager option to waive working notice with pay. Garden leave is paid no-duty no-access transition up to 1 month with approvals. Severance is 2 weeks per year of service, minimum 4 weeks, for role eliminations. Separation agreement carries standard releases with 21 days to consider and 7 to revoke. Rehire eligibility is recorded at departure and visible to future panels.

## Frequently asked questions

Can I resign by chat message? Written notice means email or portal; chat starts the clock when HR acknowledges it. What if I get a counter-offer? Off-cycle adjustments follow the Compensation Policy path with VP and HR approval. Do I keep the laptop? No, all gear returns per the Equipment Policy.

## Garden leave and transition consulting

Critical-role departures may serve garden leave (paid, no duties, no access) up to 1 month with VP and HR approval instead of working notice. Transition consulting after departure is a separate paid agreement at an agreed daily rate, never an expectation. Both arrangements are documented before the last day.

## Layoff selection and support

Selection criteria (skills matrix, tenure bands, performance standing) are set before names are discussed and reviewed for bias by HR. Affected employees get severance per this policy, 2 months placement support, reference letters on request, and benefits-continuation paperwork in the departure packet. Internal transfer windows open 2 weeks before external posting of backfilled roles.

## Non-compete and non-solicit rules

No general non-competes are required. Role-specific non-solicitation of clients (never of colleagues) may apply to sales leadership with written terms and time bounds under 1 year. Nondisclosure of restricted data survives departure indefinitely per the Data Security Policy.

## Rehire eligibility

Voluntary departures in good standing are rehire-eligible immediately. Involuntary performance cases wait 1 year with evidence of growth. Serious-misconduct terminations are not rehire-eligible. Eligibility is recorded at departure and visible to future hiring panels.

## Related policies map

Notice periods interact with PTO payout rules (accrued to 10 days, carried days excluded). Equipment return follows the Equipment Policy 2-week rule with prepaid labels. Benefits continuation uses the 30-day election window. Knowledge transfer mirrors the Onboarding handoff template in reverse. Conduct-track terminations cite the Workplace Conduct investigation record. Final expenses clear under the 30-day Expense Policy clock.
