# HR Contacts and Offices Policy

## How to reach HR

The HR portal handles requests, filings, and acknowledgments. Urgent people issues go to the HR direct line during business hours; the ethics line takes anonymous reports around the clock. Response standards follow the Approval Matrix turnarounds.

## Office locations

Headquarters hosts HR, Finance, IT, and Security leadership. Regional hubs publish their own front-desk hours in the portal. Remote employees use the same digital channels; no issue requires physical presence except equipment handoff, which ships instead.

## What goes where

Policy questions: chat assistant first, then HR portal ticket with citations attached. Benefits and payroll: HR benefits queue. Equipment and access: IT queue with same-day response for day-1 failures. Security incidents: Security on-call immediately, not the general queue. Conduct: HR direct or ethics line, never only the requester's manager.

## Ticket SLAs

Standard tickets acknowledge in 2 business days and resolve in 5. Sensitive cases acknowledge in 2 business days with investigation timelines per the Workplace Conduct Policy. Missed SLAs escalate automatically with notice to the requester.

## Escalation path

Unanswered tickets escalate one level per missed SLA. Conduct appeals allow one HR-director review. Compensation disputes go to HR then VP. No ticket is ever closed without a resolution note the requester can see.

## Frequently asked questions

Can I walk into HR? Headquarters takes walk-ins for urgent matters; otherwise book a slot. Who sees my ticket? The handling queue plus HR leadership on sensitive cases. How do I check status? The portal tracker with full history.

## Queue catalog

Benefits queue: eligibility, enrollment, claims help, retirement questions. Payroll queue: stubs, discrepancies, withholding, deposit changes. IT queue: access, equipment, incidents with day-1 failures jumping the line. Security queue: incidents only, always urgent. Conduct queue: HR direct plus ethics-line intake merged into one triage. General HR: everything else, routed within 2 business days.

## Office hours and coverage

Queues run published business hours per region with holiday calendars observed. On-call rotations cover Security incidents and building emergencies only. Planned leaves inside HR teams trigger named backup owners published in the directory, so no queue ever goes dark silently.

## Service standards reporting

Monthly service reports show volume, acknowledgment compliance, resolution compliance, and escalation counts per queue. Missed SLAs attach the corrective note. Two consecutive misses in one queue trigger a staffing review with a dated remediation plan.

## Feedback on HR itself

Rate closed tickets with the built-in survey; low scores route to queue owners, not the individual handler, first. Complaints about HR handling go to the HR director channel with the same SLA protection. Retaliation for HR feedback is a serious conduct violation.

## Published boards and calendars

The HR portal maintains four public boards: the holiday and blackout calendar (November publish), the benefits enrollment calendar (September publish with October window), the review cycle calendar (December publish), and the training calendar (quarterly). Subscription feeds exist for each; managers subscribe their teams at quarter start. Corrections publish with 1 month notice and never retroactively change taken leave or pay.

## Change communication standard

Every policy change ships with a summary of what changed, why, the effective date, and grandfathering rules where behavior already in flight is affected. Material changes allow 2 weeks of questions before taking effect; urgent safety changes take effect immediately with next-day explanation. The monthly HR note rounds up all changes with links to the full text.

## After-hours protocol

Nights and weekends: ethics line for conduct, Security on-call for incidents, manager phone tree for operational emergencies. Everything else waits for business hours; tickets filed overnight get the standard 2-business-day acknowledgment from the next business day.

## Directory structure

The portal directory lists every queue with its owner, SLA, and escalation target. Personal contact details are never published; all contact runs through queues, the direct line, or scheduled slots. New queues are announced in the monthly HR note with a 1-month parallel run of the old path.

## Records and privacy

Contact logs follow the ticket retention rules: standard 3 years, sensitive cases per the conduct policy. Requesters see their own history in full. Aggregate volumes publish quarterly with no names attached.

## Definitions glossary

Queue is the owned intake per topic with published SLA. Direct line is the business-hours urgent people-issues number. Ethics line is the around-the-clock anonymous reporting channel. Case number is the tracked identifier issued for sensitive and security matters. Parallel run is the 1-month overlap when a contact path changes, so nothing goes dark silently.
