# Leave of Absence Policy

## Parental leave

12 weeks paid parental leave for birth, adoption, or foster placement, usable within the first year. Give 1 month notice where foreseeable. Health coverage continues; PTO accrual pauses after 30 days of continuous leave. Leave may be taken continuously or in two blocks with HR agreement.

## Medical leave

Up to 12 weeks for serious health conditions with medical certification, recertified every 30 days. Pay coordination: available PTO is applied first only where the employee elects it; the remainder is unpaid unless short-term disability applies. Job restoration follows applicable law.

## Unpaid personal leave

Up to 30 days for personal reasons with manager and HR approval, usable after 1 year of service. Benefits continue if the employee pays the employee share monthly. Accrual pauses throughout. Extensions need a fresh request before expiry.

## Jury duty and civic leave

Jury duty is paid with summons documentation; remit any court stipend or keep it and take the days unpaid, employee's choice. Voting leave of 2 hours applies where polls are open less than 4 hours outside shift time. Military leave follows applicable law with full job restoration rights.

## Coordination with PTO

PTO runs concurrently with family emergency leave only where elected, and with parental or medical leave only where elected. Holiday closures during leave do not consume leave balances. See the PTO Policy and Holidays and Leave Policy for the elected-concurrency rules.

## Request process

File in the HR portal with dates, leave type, and supporting documents. HR responds within 5 business days with the determination, pay treatment, and return date. Extensions need fresh documentation before the current period ends. Denials state the reason and appeal path.

## Return to work

Return dates are confirmed 1 week ahead. Fitness-for-duty notes apply to medical returns over 2 weeks. Phased returns (reduced schedule) are available for 2 weeks with HR agreement. Missed check-ins trigger a welfare outreach, not discipline, as the first step.

## Frequently asked questions

Can parental leave be split across years? Yes, within the first year of the event. Does medical leave affect bonuses? Proration follows time active; see Compensation and Payroll. What if documents arrive late? Coverage starts on filing; backdating needs HR approval with cause.

## Certification detail

Medical certifications state the condition class (not full history), expected duration, and work restrictions. Recertification every 30 days keeps the case current. Insufficient paperwork pauses pay treatment, never the leave itself, until cured within 2 weeks of notice.

## Intermittent leave rules

Chronic conditions may use intermittent leave in day or half-day blocks with same-day notice where flare-ups are unforeseeable, or scheduled blocks where treatment dates are known. Abuse patterns (for example every Friday without clinical basis) trigger a recertification request, not discipline, as the first step.

## Worked examples

Example A: parental leave of 12 weeks starting 1 month after birth, taken continuously. Notice given 2 months prior, coverage continuous, return confirmed 1 week out.

Example B: surgery with 6 weeks medical leave, PTO elected for the first week, short-term disability covering weeks 2 to 6 after the elimination period. Recertification filed at day 30.

Example C: jury duty of 4 days with summons copy. Paid in full, stipend kept, no leave balances touched.

## Definitions glossary

Continuous leave is one unbroken span. Intermittent leave is blocks or reduced schedules for chronic conditions. Elimination period is the unpaid wait before disability benefits start. Fitness-for-duty is the clinician note clearing a medical return over 2 weeks. Phased return is the agreed reduced schedule for the first 2 weeks back. Elected concurrency is the employee's choice to run PTO alongside another leave type.

## Communication norms during leave

The employee names one contact channel and a check-in cadence at leave start. Managers send only the agreed updates, never work requests, except genuine emergencies defined in advance. Return logistics (access, equipment, coverage handoff back) are confirmed 1 week before the date.
