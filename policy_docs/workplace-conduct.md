# Workplace Conduct Policy

## Code of conduct

Treat colleagues with respect, communicate honestly, and take responsibility for mistakes. Discrimination, harassment, intimidation, and retaliation are prohibited. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR promptly. Gifts from vendors over $50 must be declined or donated.

## Harassment definition and examples

Harassment is unwelcome conduct, based on a protected characteristic or otherwise severe or pervasive, that creates a hostile environment. Examples include slurs, threats, unwanted advances, and sabotage of work. A single severe incident qualifies; so does a pattern of smaller ones. Intent does not excuse impact, though it informs the response level.

## Reporting channels

Report to your manager, HR directly, or the anonymous ethics line. Sensitive workplace issues may go straight to HR. Reports may be made by the affected person or a witness. Anonymous reports are investigated to the extent possible. No channel is ever the wrong channel in an emergency.

## Investigation process

HR acknowledges reports within 2 business days and completes investigations within 10 business days where feasible. Both sides are heard, records are kept confidential on a need-to-know basis, and interim measures (schedule or reporting changes) may apply during review. Extensions are communicated with reasons, not silence.

## Outcomes

Minor issues get coaching with a written record. Serious issues get a formal case with sanctions up to termination. Outcomes are communicated to the reporter in summary form; full records stay with HR. Coaching records expire after 1 year of clean conduct.

## Retaliation prohibited

Retaliation for good-faith reporting is itself a serious violation, investigated under the same timelines. Report suspected retaliation through any channel immediately. Good-faith reports that prove unfounded carry no penalty.

## HR triage

The assistant retrieves relevant policy for conduct questions, determines whether to answer directly or recommend escalation, and can draft a mock HR case summary. It never takes irreversible action without confirmation.

## Frequently asked questions

Can I report about my own manager? Yes, go straight to HR or the ethics line. Will the subject know who reported? Only what the investigation requires; anonymity is protected to the extent possible. What if it happens offsite? Company events and work travel are covered workplaces.

## Investigation steps in detail

Step 1: intake and interim safety check within 2 business days. Step 2: evidence gathering — documents, messages, witness accounts — with both sides heard separately. Step 3: findings against the code definitions, reviewed by a second HR partner for calibration. Step 4: outcome and summary communication, plus monitoring for retaliation for 90 days after close.

## Manager duties

Managers acknowledge reports within 1 business day, take no investigative action themselves beyond preserving evidence, implement HR-directed interim measures, and never promise confidentiality they cannot keep. They report the matter upward the same day and keep the team running normally around it.

## Worked examples

Example A: repeated demeaning comments in standup with two witnesses. Pattern qualifies; outcome is coaching with a written record and 90-day monitoring, HR triage classification minor-to-serious boundary documented.

Example B: a single threat of violence. Severe single incident; immediate interim separation, formal case, sanctions up to termination. Reporter gets summary; team gets a conduct reminder without case details.

Example C: a sensitive interpersonal complaint about a manager, filed straight to HR. Investigation runs outside the chain of command; the assistant, if asked, retrieves this policy and recommends the HR-direct route with a mock case summary option.

## Definitions glossary

Protected characteristic covers the legally protected classes plus company-added ones published yearly. Hostile environment is severe-or-pervasive unwelcome conduct a reasonable person would find abusive. Interim measures are temporary schedule or reporting changes during review. Coaching is the documented minor-outcome with expiry after 1 clean year. Formal case is the serious track with sanctions up to termination. Retaliation is adverse action for good-faith reporting, itself a serious violation.

## Records and confidentiality

Case files live in restricted HR storage with need-to-know access, retained per the case policy. Interview notes are summarized, not verbatim transcripts, to protect candor. Reporters receive outcome summaries; witnesses receive thanks without outcome detail. Aggregate case volumes publish yearly with no names or teams identified.
