# Benefits Policy

## Eligibility

Full-time employees are eligible for the Standard health plan from their start date. Part-time employees are ineligible until they convert to full-time status, at which point enrollment opens immediately with a 30-day window. HR confirms every eligibility change in writing before coverage begins.

## Enrollment windows

Enroll during onboarding or the annual open enrollment window, held each October for the following year. Life events (marriage, birth or adoption, relocation, loss of other coverage) open a 30-day special window from the event date. Missing a window means waiting for the next one; there are no exceptions outside qualifying events.

## Plan details

The Standard plan covers preventive care fully and specialist visits with copay. The plan includes dental and vision riders at no extra premium. The deductible is published yearly in the portal; the out-of-pocket maximum caps annual exposure. See the benefits portal for the full schedule of copays, deductibles, and out-of-pocket maximums.

## Choosing a plan tier

Two tiers are offered: Standard (default) and Standard Plus with lower specialist copays for a higher premium. Tier selection happens at enrollment and locks for the plan year except on qualifying life events. A comparison worksheet with worked examples lives in the portal.

## Retirement

The company matches 401(k) contributions at 50 percent up to 6 percent of salary, vesting over 3 years. Enrollment is independent of health-plan windows and may start any pay period. Contribution changes take effect within 2 pay cycles. Departing employees receive rollover paperwork automatically.

## Life event procedures

Report life events to HR within 2 weeks, with documentation, to open the 30-day special enrollment window. Acceptable documentation includes marriage certificates, birth records, and leases or bills proving relocation. HR issues the updated confirmation before the window closes.

## Leave coordination

Health coverage continues during paid leave. During unpaid leave beyond 30 days, employees may continue coverage by paying the employee share monthly; missed payments lapse coverage with 15 days written notice. See the Leave of Absence Policy for details.

## Wellness benefit

An annual $300 wellness stipend covers gym, classes, or preventive programs, claimed with receipts through expenses. It does not roll over. Mental-health visits follow the Standard plan copay schedule with no session cap.

## Frequently asked questions

Do domestic partners qualify? Yes, with affidavit plus shared-residence proof. Does coverage start day one? Yes for full-time hires, including onboarding month. Who do I call with a denied claim? HR benefits first, then the carrier appeal process documented in the portal.

## Claims and appeals

File claims through the carrier portal with the visit receipt. Denied claims get a written reason with the plan section cited. First appeal goes to the carrier within 60 days; second appeal goes to HR benefits with the full record. Emergency care is never denied for lack of pre-authorization.

## Dependent rules

Spouses, domestic partners (affidavit plus shared-residence proof), and children to age 26 are eligible dependents. Newborns are covered from birth when reported within 30 days. Dependent verification runs every 2 years; ineligible dependents found in audit are removed prospectively, never punitively billed backward.

## Continuation after departure

Departing employees may continue health coverage at full premium plus administration fee for up to 12 months, elected within 30 days of departure. Election paperwork ships with the final paycheck. Conversion to individual plans is supported with carrier contact sheets.

## Roles and responsibilities

The employee owns timely enrollment and life-event reporting. HR owns eligibility confirmation and window administration. Managers own nothing in benefits except approving time to attend enrollment sessions. The carrier owns claims decisions subject to the appeal ladder above.

## Administration calendar

January: new-plan-year confirmations ship with updated premium and deductible figures. October: open enrollment with comparison workshops and 1-on-1 slots. November: confirmations and ID cards. December: year-end contribution statements. Life events run their own 30-day clocks independent of this cadence.

## Definitions glossary

Premium is the monthly price of coverage, shared between company and employee. Deductible is what the employee pays before the plan contributes (preventive care exempt). Copay is the fixed per-visit price. Out-of-pocket maximum caps yearly exposure including deductible and copays. Qualifying life event is the legal trigger opening special enrollment. Vesting is the schedule on which matching contributions become the employee's property. Special enrollment is the 30-day window outside October. Continuation is the post-departure full-premium option elected within 30 days.

## Worked scenarios

Scenario A: October hire enrolls during onboarding, picks Standard, adds a spouse with affidavit. Coverage day one, first premium on the November stub with the worksheet attached.

Scenario B: March birth. HR receives the report plus certificate in week 2, opens the 30-day special window, newborn covered from the birth date, premiums adjusted next cycle.

Scenario C: part-time Analyst converts to full-time in June. HR confirms in writing, 30-day window opens, Standard plan starts the next pay period with dental and vision riders included.

## New-hire enrollment walkthrough

Week 1: benefits orientation with the comparison worksheet and tier choice explained. Week 2: portal enrollment with dependent documents uploaded. Week 3: confirmation statement reviewed for accuracy — tier, dependents, deductions. Errors found in week 3 correct retroactively to day one; later errors correct prospectively from discovery. HR runs open office hours through every onboarding month.
