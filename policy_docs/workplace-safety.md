# Workplace Safety Policy

## General duty

Everyone keeps the workplace safe: report hazards immediately, never bypass guards or interlocks, and stop work you reasonably believe is dangerous. Managers own hazard correction in their areas with dated action plans.

## Emergency procedures

Fire: alarm, evacuate by the posted route, assemble at the marked point, never re-enter. Medical: call emergency services, then building response, then the manager. Severe weather: follow the warden's direction to shelter areas. Drills run twice yearly; attendance is mandatory with makeup sessions within 2 weeks.

## Ergonomics

Workstations follow the ergonomic setup guide in the IT portal. Assessments are available on request with adjustments implemented within 3 weeks. Remote workers use the same guide; chairs and desks follow the Equipment and Expense Policy path. Reported pain gets an assessment within 1 week, never a wait-and-see.

## Mental health and EAP

The employee assistance program offers confidential counseling with no session cap under the Standard plan copay schedule. Managers may suggest, never mandate, EAP contact. Crisis resources are posted in every office and the portal. Time for appointments follows the civic-appointments rule.

## Incident reporting

Report injuries, near misses, and hazards in the safety portal the same day. Investigations complete within 5 business days with corrective actions assigned owners and dates. Retaliation for reporting is a serious conduct violation. Serious injuries trigger immediate leadership notification and a 24-hour preliminary review.

## Visitors and contractors

Visitors sign in, wear badges, and stay with their host in non-public areas. Contractors follow the site safety briefing before starting work. Deliveries use the loading route only.

## Records

Incident reports, drill attendance, assessments, and corrective actions are retained 4 years. Anonymized safety stats publish quarterly. Medical details stay in confidential files with restricted access.

## Frequently asked questions

What if my manager ignores a hazard? Report to Safety or HR directly; escalation is protected. Do remote workers count? Yes for ergonomics and EAP; physical emergency procedures apply to offices. Are drills paid time? Yes, scheduled inside work hours with coverage arranged.

## Hazard correction workflow

Hazards enter the safety portal with location, photo where helpful, and severity guess. Safety triages within 2 business days: imminent danger gets same-day action, serious items get dated plans inside 2 weeks, minor items queue into maintenance. Reporters see status changes; closure needs a verification note, not just a checkbox.

## Office-specific rules

Kitchens follow posted cleaning rotations; unlabeled fridge items clear weekly. Server and electrical rooms stay locked with access lists. Standing desks default to the ergonomic guide heights on install day. Parking areas follow the 5 mph limit with pedestrian priority; bike storage is provided, not guaranteed against theft.

## Travel and event safety

Offsites get a safety brief covering venue exits, local emergency numbers, and the incident contact chain. High-risk destination travel needs the Security briefing per the Travel Policy. Event organizers file a 1-page safety plan for gatherings over 50 people.

## Safety committee

The committee meets monthly with rotating membership across functions, reviews all open corrective actions, and publishes minutes. Any employee may add agenda items. Committee recommendations get a dated leadership response within 1 month.

## Definitions glossary

Hazard is any condition that could cause harm, reported same-day in the safety portal. Near miss is the unplanned event that could have caused harm and is investigated like an incident. Corrective action is the assigned fix with owner and date, verified at closure. Imminent danger triggers same-day action. Assembly point is the marked post-evacuation gathering location. Warden is the floor lead directing drills and real evacuations.
