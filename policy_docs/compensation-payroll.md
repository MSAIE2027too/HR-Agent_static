# Compensation and Payroll Policy

## Pay schedule

Pay is semi-monthly on the 15th and last business day. Direct deposit is standard; pay stubs live in the HR portal. Report discrepancies within 30 days for correction in the next cycle. Off-cycle checks issue only for errors over $500.

## Overtime

Non-exempt employees earn 1.5 times the hourly rate beyond 40 hours per week, pre-approved by the manager. Exempt employees receive no overtime. Misclassification concerns go to HR for review. Unapproved overtime is still paid and then addressed as a conduct matter.

## Bonuses

Annual performance bonuses are decided in the review cycle against published goals, paid in the first quarter. Proration applies to mid-year joiners. Bonuses are discretionary and require active employment on payout date. Bonus targets are communicated in writing at the cycle start.

## Performance reviews

Reviews run annually with goal-setting in Q1, a midpoint check-in, and ratings in Q4. Ratings feed bonus and promotion decisions. Improvement plans last 3 months with written milestones and biweekly check-ins; remote-work eligibility pauses during a plan.

## Payroll changes

Address, withholding, and deposit changes take effect within 2 pay cycles of HR-portal submission. Mid-cycle corrections appear as adjustments, never as off-cycle checks except for errors over $500. Year-end tax documents ship by the legal deadline.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved expense reports reimburse within 2 pay cycles per the Expense Policy. Travel advances clear against the filed report within the same window. Payroll never withholds reimbursement over unrelated disputes.

## Salary reviews

Base pay is reviewed annually with market benchmarking. Off-cycle adjustments need VP and HR approval for scope changes, counter-offers, or equity corrections. Salary bands are shared with employees on request.

## Frequently asked questions

When do new hires get first pay? The next cycle after start, prorated daily. How are partial months handled? Daily proration against scheduled workdays. Who sees my pay data? Payroll staff and your manager's budget view only.

## Review timeline in detail

January: bands refreshed with market data. February: managers draft ratings and bonus recommendations. March: calibration sessions finalize distribution. Payouts land in the first quarter with itemized statements showing base, bonus, and the goal linkage. Appeals on factual errors only, within 2 weeks of payout.

## Promotion criteria

Promotions need sustained next-level performance across two cycles, a business need for the role, and calibration approval. Scope-change adjustments outside the cycle need VP and HR approval. Promotion letters state the new band, effective date, and review timing.

## Worked examples

Example A: an Engineer meeting all goals with one standout delivery earns the standard bonus multiplier with the goal linkage attached. Example B: mid-year joiner at 50 percent of the cycle gets a prorated award with the proration math shown. Example C: approved overtime of 5 hours at $40 hourly pays $300 with manager pre-approval on file.

## Records

Pay records, ratings, and bonus statements are retained 4 years. Employees may request their own history anytime; it ships within 5 business days. Calibration notes stay with HR and are never pasted into personnel files verbatim.

## Definitions glossary

Base pay is the fixed salary reviewed annually against market bands. Bonus target is the communicated percentage tied to published goals, paid in Q1. Proration scales awards to time active for mid-year joiners. Off-cycle adjustment is any out-of-band pay change needing VP and HR approval. Daily proration divides monthly pay by scheduled workdays for partial months.

## Worked scenarios

Scenario A: a July joiner at 50 percent of the cycle with met goals receives half the target award with the proration math itemized on the statement. Scenario B: a scope-change promotion in September carries an off-cycle adjustment with VP and HR signatures and the new band stated. Scenario C: a $600 payroll error found within 30 days corrects as an off-cycle check since it exceeds the $500 threshold.

## Compensation communication norms

Managers discuss pay in bands, not peer comparisons: role level, market position, and growth path are shareable; colleagues' exact figures are not the manager's to disclose. Offer letters state band placement rationale in one line so future reviews start from shared facts. Counter-offer conversations happen once per event with HR present; repeated bidding rounds are declined as policy. Salary history is never requested from candidates; band placement rests on role scope and market evidence. All-hands share aggregate band movement yearly with no individual data.
