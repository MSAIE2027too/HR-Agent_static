# Travel Policy

## Booking

Book travel through the company portal at least 2 weeks in advance. Flights default to economy; flights over 6 hours may book premium economy with manager approval. Hotels cap at the city rate published quarterly. Changes within 72 hours of departure need manager re-approval.

## Per diem

Meals are covered at $75 per day with receipts; no per diem is paid without travel. Lodging follows the city cap; overages need Finance pre-approval. Ground transport uses standard ride tiers; rentals need manager approval. Alcohol follows the Expense Policy client-entertainment rule.

## International travel

International trips need manager plus Finance approval and a tax-location review when the stay exceeds 30 days. Confirm right-to-work and visa requirements 1 month ahead. Security briefing is mandatory for high-risk destinations. Carry printed approvals and emergency contacts.

## Combining travel with remote work

Trip extensions for remote work follow the Remote Work Policy: the 6-week annual cap includes extension days, and manager plus Security sign-off are required before booking the return change. Tax-location rules apply to the extended stay. The extension request rides with the original trip approval.

## Reimbursement

File within 30 days per the Expense Policy. Amounts of $500 or more need Finance approval in addition to the manager. Reimbursement lands within 2 pay cycles. Advances clear against the filed report in the same window.

## Cancellations

Cancel unused bookings immediately; no-show fees without documented cause are charged back. Trip insurance is included on portal bookings. Rebooked personal extensions are at the employee's expense except the approved remote-work portion.

## Frequently asked questions

Can I extend a trip over a weekend personally? Yes, at your own lodging cost, with the return flight unchanged. Do red-eyes count as a travel day? Yes for per-diem purposes with manager acknowledgment.

## Traveling with equipment

Company laptops travel in cabin baggage, never checked. Encrypted backups run before departure. Border crossings follow the clean-device guidance in the security briefing: minimal data on the device, full sync on return. Loss abroad follows the 24-hour report rule with remote wipe.

## Team travel administration

Trip owners consolidate bookings, per-diem advances, and receipts per traveler while keeping individual accountability: each traveler files their own report against the shared itinerary. Group ground transport beats reimbursed solo rides on cost and is the default.

## Visa and documentation support

HR provides invitation letters within 5 business days of the approved request. Passport validity must exceed 6 months beyond return for most destinations; check the briefing sheet. Visa fees and required photos are reimbursable with receipts under the standard thresholds.

## Post-trip closeout

Reports file within 30 days with the itinerary attached. Trip retrospectives for customer visits go to the account record within 1 week. Unused advances clear in the same window; overruns need the documented cause attached.

## Definitions glossary

Per diem is the $75 daily meals allowance on travel days with receipts. City cap is the quarterly published lodging ceiling per destination. Premium economy needs manager approval on flights over 6 hours. Trip owner is the single accountable person for group travel cost tracking. Personal extension is the employee-funded portion of a lengthened trip, itemized separately at booking.

## Pre-trip checklist

Approvals filed 2 weeks out; visas and right-to-work confirmed 1 month out for international; security briefing completed for high-risk destinations; equipment backup run; emergency contacts and approvals printed; per-diem advance requested if needed. The portal blocks ticket issuance until approvals land, so last-minute scrambles surface early. What about conferences? Registration plus travel follows the same approvals; proceedings sharing is expected afterward.

## Booking classes in detail

Economy is standard; premium economy needs manager approval on flights over 6 hours; business class needs VP approval and a documented reason such as medical need or a same-day board meeting. Rail replaces short-haul flights under 4 hours where available. Loyalty points earned on company travel belong to the employee; status challenges may not drive booking choices.

## Incidents abroad

Medical emergency: local emergency services first, then the travel-assistance line in the portal, then the manager. Lost passport: consulate plus manager notification the same day. Missed connections from delays need no approval to rebook within the same class. Security events follow the Data Security Policy on-call path.

## Delegation travel

Managers traveling with reports book together and approve on the trip. Interview candidates travel economy with the same per-diem rules. Group offsites need a single trip owner, a published agenda, and per-person cost tracking against one cost center.

## Records

Itineraries, approvals, receipts, and incident notes attach to the trip record for 3 years. Finance samples international trips quarterly for tax-review compliance. Personal extension costs are itemized separately at booking so splits stay clean.
